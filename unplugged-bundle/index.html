Questions?

 

What's the difference between the two packs?

Explorer (11–14) is competition and high-energy group games. Trailblazer (15–17) is autonomy, leadership, and advanced skills. Totally different energy, zero overlap — every single challenge is unique.

What if I only have one age group?

Then you probably only need one pack. Explorer ($5) for 11–14, Trailblazer ($7) for 15–17. The bundle makes sense when you have both ages, or a teen right on the edge.

Is this a physical product?

No — digital download. You get two web pages (one per pack) that you open in any browser and print. Print as many copies as you need.

What language is it in?

Every card has both English and Spanish instructions. Works perfectly in bilingual families.

Do I need special gear?

Almost nothing beyond what you already bring camping. A few challenges use specific tools, but the cards tell you exactly what's needed for each one.

My teen thinks everything is "lame." Will this work?

Each pack includes a parent guide with a proven strategy for getting buy-in from even the most resistant teen. Hint: you never announce it as an "activity." Competition beats any lecture.

Only need one age group?

Each pack is also available separately.

¿Y si solo tengo un grupo de edad?

Entonces probablemente solo necesitas un pack. Explorer ($5) para 11–14, Trailblazer ($7) para 15–17. El bundle tiene sentido cuando tienes ambas edades, o un adolescente justo en el límite.

¿Es un producto físico?

No — descarga digital. Recibes dos páginas web (una por pack) que abres en cualquier navegador e imprimes. Imprime tantas copias como necesites.

¿En qué idioma está?

¿Necesito equipo especial?

Casi nada fuera de lo que ya llevas al campamento. Algunos retos usan herramientas específicas, pero las tarjetas te dicen exactamente qué necesitas para cada uno.
