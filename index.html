Meet Paola 👋

Hi, I'm Paola. I didn't grow up doing this, I found the outdoors as an adult and fell in love with the feeling of trying new things and learning as I go. My husband is my adventure partner, and so much of what I know I've learned with him and our family by my side. These days that also means a garden out back, sourdough on the counter, and cooking real, whole food, old-fashioned on purpose. I share my real process, the wins and the awkward first tries, because I believe the world would be a better place if we all spent more time outside and closer to the simple things. If I could start from zero, so can you. 🌲

Hola, soy Paola 👋

No creci haciendo esto: descubri el aire libre de adulta y me enamore de probar cosas nuevas y aprender sobre la marcha. Mi esposo es mi companero de aventuras, y mucho de lo que se lo aprendi con el y con mi familia a mi lado. Hoy eso tambien significa un huerto atras, masa madre en la cocina y cocinar comida real, a la antigua a proposito. Comparto mi proceso real, los logros y los primeros intentos torpes, porque creo que el mundo seria mejor si todos pasaramos mas tiempo afuera y mas cerca de lo simple. Si yo pude empezar de cero, tu tambien. 🌲
