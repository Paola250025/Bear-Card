FAQ

I know NOTHING about mushrooms. Is this guide for me?
It was made for you. Written so someone who has never identified a mushroom can start from zero. Every species has a full visual description, its dangerous look-alike, and where to find it. No technical jargon.

Can I print just some pages?
Yes. The guide is organized so you find your region, see which mushrooms grow there, and print only those ID cards. You don't need to carry the whole guide on the trail.

Does this replace an expert or a foraging course?
No — and no digital product should promise that. This guide gives you the foundation to recognize 10 key species and know what to do when you find them. To forage and eat wild mushrooms confidently, I always recommend supplementing with a local foraging group or mycologist. The guide includes a full safety disclaimer.

What format does it come in?
Digital PDF you can view on any device (phone, tablet, computer) and also print. You get the Spanish version and the English version.

Does it work outside the US?
Some of the species covered (chanterelle, porcini, lion's mane) are cosmopolitan and found in many countries. However, the regional tables and seasons are based on US regions.

Safety notice: This guide is for educational purposes. Never eat a wild mushroom based solely on a written guide. When in doubt, leave it alone. If you suspect poisoning, call emergency services immediately. Always consult an experienced forager before consuming wild mushrooms.
